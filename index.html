Key Features

Dynamic CSV Menus

Manage your items, prices, and categories simply by editing a standard menu.csv file. Change prices or add inventory on the fly.

Flexible Grid Layouts

Choose between standard, medium, or high-density button layouts to best suit touchscreens or compact displays.

Custom Touch Keypad

Integrated on-screen touch keypad for fast cash tender entry, with support for left, center, right, or disabled placement.

Sales & Reporting

Automatically records transactions to organized CSV files. Generate instantaneous sales summary reports with itemized revenue breakdowns.

Built-In Practice Mode

Train volunteers or staff risk-free. Practice mode isolates test sales into a separate file without affecting real business totals.

Configurable Sales Tax

Easily toggle tax calculation on or off and set custom tax percentages in the system configuration menu.

Interface Showcase

Clean Standard Layout

Large, high-contrast touch buttons designed for quick item selection during fast service hours.

Integrated Touch Keypad

Optional side keypad panel for fast, accurate cash tender entry without needing a hardware keyboard.

Instant Change Calculation

Live order updates, automatically calculated tax, clear total displays, and large change readouts.
